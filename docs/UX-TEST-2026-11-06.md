# AgriRover: UX test, the problems it solves, and the five steps (6 Nov 2026)

Tested in the browser on the demo stack (localhost:5173) at 1024 px and at phone width (375 px). Every main screen and tab was opened; buttons, menus, dialogs, form checks, a full rover round and keyboard focus were tried. 292 API tests and 20 web tests pass; the browser console showed no errors.

Changed during the test: one simulated round on G1 (it is now today's round for G1). "Pinch new shoots" on G2 was marked done to test it, then put back in the database.

## What the system is

A guava farm's daily decision tool, built around a rover. Once a day the rover visits every tree, reads soil and air, and looks at the leaves. The app turns each round into one list for the day: water per tree, whether to feed, which trees to look at, and the stage tasks that are due (bagging, foliar sprays, pruning). Around that list it keeps the plan by date, a photo check for sick plants, and cost and sales per block.

Everything is simulated so far: wheels, camera, probe, orchard and two weeks of rounds. The screens say so.

## The problems it solves

| Pain | Evidence | What AgriRover does | Proven? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wrong water at the wrong stage.** Too little at bloom drops flowers; too much brings cracking and root problems | Deficit at bloom and fruit growth costs fruit (see [GUAVA-RESEARCH.md](GUAVA-RESEARCH.md)) | Litres per tree from that tree's own reading and the block's stage; holds water during induction stress on purpose | Simulated only |
| **Fertiliser by guesswork** | Cambodian synthetic fertiliser use rose from about 21 to 49 kg/ha in 2016-2021, and most farmers get no dose advice ([Cambojanews op-ed, World Bank figures](https://cambojanews.com/op-ed-enhancing-agricultural-services-would-help-farmers-curb-excessive-use-of-chemical-fertilizers/)) | Feed from the stage plan, held back when soil EC is high, corrected by a leaf test. Never from a cheap N-P-K probe | Rules built; no field result |
| **Fruit fly** | 20-25 % yield loss and infestation in 97 % of surveyed guava orchards in Bangladesh ([SAU archive](https://archive.saulibrary.edu.bd/handle/123456789/3773)). Bagged fruit 1.3-17 % infested, against 67 % unbagged, in a West Bengal trial ([KrishiKosh](https://krishikosh.egranth.ac.in/items/f0cd3c4e-f0ec-4626-bf78-02644a1dceca/full)) | "Bag the fruit at 3-4 cm" task, tied to a measured fruit size, plus a fruit-fly-weather warning | The task works; timing value not measured |
| **Disease seen too late** | Anthracnose is a main post-harvest disease of guava; one Brazilian abstract puts post-harvest losses at 40-60 % ([SciELO](https://scielo.br/j/tpp/a/YgK6gS34X3BPJ3FgRwWcdNk/?lang=en)) | Yellow-leaf flags per tree, a photo check that screens healthy or problem, a disease guide, and a photo library for an expert to confirm | Triage only: right about 4 in 10 on naming |
| **No agronomist to ask** | Extension staff report too little capacity to help farmers (same Cambojanews piece) | One list a day in plain words, with "Ask an agronomist" on every chemical dose | Not tested with a farmer |
| **Hours walking rows, scarce labour** | Rural youth migration and rising wages ([agrirover-business-review.md](../agrirover-business-review.md)) | The rover does the walk | No rover body yet |
| **Not knowing if a block makes money** | Same review: growers pay per hectare, so the per-block cost is what sells | Cost, harvest, sales and cost per kg by block | Sample figures only |

These figures come from other crops' fields and other countries. None of them is AgriRover's result, so in a pitch they describe the size of the problem, not what AgriRover saves.

## The five steps, applied

**1. Question every requirement: who asked?** Every row in `PLAN.md` is owned by Daivai. No grower outside the team has asked for any of it yet. The question the rover must answer is the plan's own: *do trees in one block really differ day to day?* If they don't, one fixed probe per block gives the same advice for far less.

**2. Delete.** Items on screen that answer none of the farmer's questions today:
- N·P·K on every block card: shown on purpose, never used, and labelled "Not rated" five times. Remove it from Today; keep it in the rover's tree table.
- Water rows in the Today list repeat the water line on each block card. Keep one.
- Farm › Stock and Equipment are empty in the demo; Compare blocks is mostly "Not counted" until there is a harvest. Hide them from the showcase path.
- Devices shows the rover as "not commissioned" while the Rover screen says "online". Remove one of the two, or make them agree.

**3. Simplify.** Today should open on the five most urgent actions, with the rest folded below. Status words should mean one thing: "Now", "Overdue", "Check" and "Due" sit side by side today.

**4. Accelerate.** The fastest proof is not the rover. It is the hand-carried round already in `PLAN.md`: one person walks the probe from tree to tree for two weeks and types the readings in. That needs a single screen, and it settles step 1. For the showcase, one "Run today's round" for all five blocks replaces five runs with five checklists.

**5. Automate last.** The Telegram daily message and any valve control come after a grower acts on the list and trusts it. That order is already in the plan, and the app keeps it: "Nothing here opens a valve."

## UX test: what worked

- Every menu item, tab and link opens the right page; no dead ends.
- The rover round: a pre-start safety checklist gates the Start button, the map animates tree by tree, and Today picks up the new findings within seconds (a new "Look at 2 trees with a small canopy" task appeared).
- Dialogs close on Escape, the backdrop and Close; empty required fields are caught. Deleting a cost, a sale, a photo, a crop or equipment asks first.
- Keyboard: visible focus ring and a sensible order. Task details are real expandable elements, so they work with the keyboard and screen readers.
- Phone: no sideways scrolling, a bottom bar with all seven places, tables fold into short cards, and the photo button opens the camera.
- Honesty is everywhere it should be: simulated rounds, sample figures, "Triage, not a diagnosis", "Ask an agronomist".

## UX test: problems, worst first

**Fixed the same day and checked in the browser:** 1, 3, 5, 6, 8 and 9. Today shows "Rover today: 1 of 5 blocks"; "Done" shows "Done: <task> · Undo", backed by a new `DELETE /cycles/{id}/orchard/jobs/{job}/done`; Devices reads "simulator online" for the rover; during stress the Rover screen says "Held: stress". Still open: 2, 4, 7 and 10-14.

**Photo result redesigned (same day):** the answer is now a card: "Problem found" and what the model saw, then tabs for "Most likely" and "Or", each with "Looks like" in a few words, two or three numbered steps, and the agronomist line when the sources lean on a spray. The short wording lives beside each guide entry (`CARDS` in `guava_diseases.py`) and says nothing the entry does not. The soil check now follows the best match only, which removes the "Guava rust under a brown-spot photo" part of 7; the matches themselves are still the model's.
